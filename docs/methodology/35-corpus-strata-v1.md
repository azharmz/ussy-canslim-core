# #35 Corpus Strata v1

Date: 2026-09-13
Status: PREREGISTERED

The independent #35 corpus should cover, where naturally available:

- all four core production patterns;
- RECOGNIZED, AMBIGUOUS, and REJECTED states;
- no-cross, first-cross, and already-crossed chronology;
- breakout-volume states below and at-or-above the frozen 1.40x threshold;
- C/A/L/M pass and non-pass states;
- missing or not-evaluable evidence;
- gap-through-pivot cases.

Unavailable strata are reported explicitly rather than manufactured.
